TRANSFORMER-BASED LANGUAGE MODELING

Toxic Comment
Classification

A fine-tuned DistilBERT language model designed for multi-label classification of six forms of harmful language in Wikipedia Talk Page comments.

DistilBERTPyTorchHugging FaceGoogle Colab

1 · THE PROBLEM

Moderation needs more than a simple toxic-or-not label.

Online communities need to recognize different kinds of harmful behavior without unnecessarily limiting discussion. The task was to build a multi-headed classifier that could distinguish toxicity, severe toxicity, obscenity, threats, insults, and identity-based hate in highly imbalanced comment data.

01Toxic
02Severe toxic
03Obscene
04Threat
05Insult
06Identity hate

2 · THE SOLUTION

Fine-tuning a pretrained transformer language model

Prepare

Cleaned Wiki text, normalized whitespace, replaced URLs and emails, tokenized comments, and addressed severe class imbalance with oversampling.

Compare

Evaluated BERT, RoBERTa, and DistilBERT, selecting DistilBERT for its balance of predictive performance and computational efficiency.

Optimize

Fine-tuned DistilBERT using Focal Loss, AdamW, learning-rate scheduling, cross-validation, and GPU acceleration to improve minority-class learning and control overfitting.

MODEL RESULT

98%Mean column-wise ROC AUC

The fine-tuned DistilBERT classifier achieved a reported ROC AUC of 0.98 across the toxicity labels, demonstrating strong ability to rank toxic examples across multiple categories.

3 · THE RESULT

A more targeted foundation for content moderation

By separating harmful behavior into six categories, the solution can support more precise moderation decisions—helping platforms respond to the most serious behaviors while preserving productive conversation.
