1 · THE PROBLEM

Which products create value—and which require closer review?

Knicks & Knacks, a multi-product retail business in this academic case study, needed a clearer way to manage 3,281 products and 3,790 SKUs. Management wanted to understand demand, profitability, product trends, geographic markets, and customer purchasing behavior.

2 · THE SOLUTION

A connected path from portfolio health to product-level drivers

Monitor

Compared percentage of items sold, average items per invoice, and average gross profit per invoice with management targets.

Diagnose

Built sortable product rankings, demand sparklines, KPI contribution measures, invoice counts, and 60/360-day profit comparisons.

Explore

Connected product selection to a geographic market view, detailed statistics, gross-profit trends, and repeat-customer activity.

3 · THE RESULT

One dashboard for monitoring, diagnosis, and market analysis

The completed Tableau experience reduced the need to assemble separate reports. Users could move from portfolio-level targets to specific products, compare recent and longer-term performance, and investigate how markets and customer activity affected each item’s role in the portfolio.
